Case study 02 · Solar energy operations

generate energy. store it smarter. sell it better.

unbottle. helps solar operators connect production, battery performance, energy-selling decisions and maintenance data to improve operational visibility and commercial decision-making.

The bottleneck

Generating electricity is only half the equation.

The other half is deciding what to do with it.

Solar operators face a moving target. Production changes with the weather. Electricity prices fluctuate. Batteries introduce decisions about when to charge and discharge. Equipment failures can reduce generation, disrupt operations and increase maintenance costs.

When production, storage, market and maintenance data sit in separate systems, it becomes harder to see where value is being lost — or where it can be recovered.

The problem isn't a lack of data. It's the gap between operational data and commercial decisions.

The fix

One view, from panel to profit.

We designed a data-driven framework to bring operational and commercial information together, helping the operator identify bottlenecks and evaluate opportunities across its solar portfolio.

  1. 01

    Connect the operation

    We established a framework for consolidating data from solar generation, battery systems, market prices, weather information and maintenance records. The goal: one clearer view of how assets perform and how that performance affects the business.

  2. 02

    Improve selling decisions

    We designed analytical processes to compare generation patterns with electricity-market movements and assess alternative selling scenarios. By combining expected production, market signals and operational constraints, the operator can evaluate when to sell energy and when alternative strategies may offer greater value.

  3. 03

    Get more from battery storage

    We developed an analytical approach to assess charging and depletion patterns against production forecasts, market prices, storage capacity and battery degradation. This supports better-informed charging and discharge schedules, balancing potential trading value with efficiency losses, operating constraints and long-term battery health.

  4. 04

    Spot maintenance bottlenecks

    We designed processes to analyze recurring breakdowns, maintenance costs and asset-performance patterns. The result is a stronger basis for prioritizing interventions, identifying potential warning signs and reducing reliance on reactive repairs.

  5. 05

    Connect operations to financial reporting

    We structured reporting around production, selling performance, battery utilization, maintenance expenditure and asset availability. Management can use this framework to compare parks, investigate underperformance and understand how technical issues translate into commercial consequences.

  6. 06

    Plan for changing conditions

    We introduced a scenario-based approach to evaluate the potential effects of weather, market-price movements, battery strategies and equipment downtime. Instead of relying on a single forecast, the operator can compare possible outcomes and make decisions with a clearer view of the trade-offs.

The result

A clearer route from energy data to commercial value.

The framework connects technical performance with the decisions that affect revenue, operating costs and asset reliability.

It creates a practical foundation for improving energy-selling efficiency, battery utilization, maintenance planning and portfolio reporting.

Financial gains will depend on the operator's starting point, market exposure, storage configuration and implementation.

The takeaway

Every megawatt-hour has a cost.

Every battery cycle has consequences. Every breakdown can affect revenue.

unbottle. helps solar operators connect these moving parts, identify the bottlenecks and make better-informed decisions about when to sell, when to store and when to maintain.

The opportunity isn't just to generate energy. It's to manage the entire operation with greater clarity and control.
