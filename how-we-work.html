Every business jams somewhere different, but the same few places come up again and again. Here is where we usually look, what we build, and the numbers we hold ourselves to.

Finance & back office

Invoice approval

  1. Received
  2. Review
  3. Approve
  4. Paid
Where it jams
Invoices wait in one person's inbox while they check each line against the order by hand.
What we build
AI reads each invoice, matches it to the order and delivery, and approves the clean ones. People only see the exceptions.
What we aim for
Approvals in hours, not days.

Month-end close

Where it jams
The team spends the first week of every month matching bank lines to the ledger in spreadsheets.

Sales & customer service

Quotes

  1. Request
  2. Price
  3. Approve
  4. Send
Where it jams
Each quote is priced from scratch by the one person who knows the price list, so customers wait and go elsewhere.
What we build
A quote assistant that drafts from the request, the price list and similar past deals. Your team checks and sends.
What we aim for
Same-day quotes, every time.
